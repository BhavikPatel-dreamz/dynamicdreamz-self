# Visual Capture Note: Shopify CRO Agency

**Route**: `/shopify-cro-agency`
**Live URL**: `https://www.dynamicdreamz.com/shopify-cro-agency/`
**Capture Date**: 2026-08-19

## Page Overview & Structure
The `/shopify-cro-agency` landing page is designed for Shopify and Shopify Plus merchants looking to optimize conversion rates, reduce cart abandonment, and increase revenue from their existing traffic through data-driven CRO audits, user behavior analysis, and A/B testing.

### Sections (in visual order):
1. **Hero Section (`.white_label_hero_section`)**:
   - Split layout with cream background (`#FAF8F5`) and rounded bottom border (`rounded-b-[50px]`).
   - Left column: H1 with italic red accent "Shopify CRO:", 4 descriptive paragraphs, and 2 CTA buttons ("Get a CRO Assessment" linking to Google Forms + "Request a Quote" linking to `/request-quote`).
   - Right column: WebP CRO banner illustration (`/assets/shopify-cro-agency/hero/shopify-cro-banner.webp`).
2. **Common Conversion Barriers (`.conversion-cro-section`)**:
   - Section heading "Common Conversion Barriers We Help Identify & Optimize" + descriptive paragraph.
   - 8 conversion barrier cards (4 columns desktop, 3 tablet, 2 mobile, 1 small mobile): Poor Mobile Experience, Weak Product Pages, Missing Trust Signals, Cart Abandonment, Confusing Navigation, Slow Store Performance, Ineffective Calls-to-Action, Poor Checkout Experience.
   - Card styling: White card, rounded 15px, light border, 56x62.5px icon container with `#AD51511C` background and custom border-radius.
3. **Revenue Impact Comparison (`.revenue-impact-section`)**:
   - Dark background (`#171E16`) with rounded bottom border (`rounded-b-[50px]`).
   - Section title "The Revenue Impact of Shopify CRO" + subtitle.
   - Side-by-side comparison cards:
     - "Before Optimization": 50,000+ Visitors, 1.0% Conversion, $100 AOV = $50,000 Revenue.
     - "After Optimization": 50,000+ Visitors, 1.5% Conversion, $100 AOV = $75,000 Revenue (green highlighted border, glowing badge, and trend indicator).
   - Bottom footer: "Additional Revenue Potential: **+$25,000** Per Month".
4. **Our Shopify CRO Services (`.shopify-cro-services`)**:
   - 5 service cards (3 on row 1, 2 centered on row 2) with divider borders: Shopify CRO Audit, Conversion Funnel Analysis, User Behavior Analysis, A/B Testing & Experimentation, Ongoing CRO Optimization.
5. **Get Your Shopify CRO Assessment (`.shopify-cro-assessment`)**:
   - Light blue-gray background (`#E6ECF0`) with rounded top border (`rounded-t-[50px]`).
   - Centered title, list of 5 assessment pills with red arrows (Monthly Revenue, Traffic Volume, Conversion Rate, Average Order Value, Analytics Setup), and "COMPLETE CRO ASSESSMENT" CTA button.
6. **Our Shopify CRO Process (`.white_label_process_step_box_section.column-five`)**:
   - 5 horizontal step boxes with connecting dashed lines: 01 Discover, 02 Analyze, 03 Prioritize, 04 Test & Implement, 05 Scale & Optimize.
7. **Why Dynamic Dreamz (`.shopify-cro-dynamic-dreamz`)**:
   - Beige container (`#F7F4E9`) with rounded 30px corners.
   - Left side: "Why Dynamic Dreamz" copy explaining strategy + development together.
   - Right side: White rounded box with 6 key credential checkmarks.
   - Bottom red strip (`#AD5151`): "One Partner. One Team. One Growth Strategy".
8. **Flexible Shopify CRO Engagements (`.shopify-cro-engagement-section`)**:
   - Light gray background (`#FAFAF7`).
   - 2 engagement cards: "Shopify CRO Audit" and "Shopify CRO Growth Partner" with divider line, bullet list, and CTA buttons.
9. **Frequently Asked Questions (`.faq-sec.bg-sky-blue`)**:
   - Blue-gray background (`#EBF5F8`).
   - 6 comprehensive CRO FAQs with collapsible accordion items.
10. **Bottom CTA Banner (`.white_label_lets_build_cta_section`)**:
    - Light gray background (`#FAFAF7`).
    - H2 "Turn Existing Traffic into More Revenue" + dual CTAs ("Get a CRO Assessment" & "Request a Quote").

## Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>=1200px)**: Split hero, 4-col barriers, 2-col revenue comparison, 3+2 CRO services grid, 5-col process flow, 2-col engagement cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: Stacked hero, 3-col barriers, 2-col revenue comparison, stacked services, 3-col process, 2-col engagement.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: Stacked hero with centered text, 2-col/1-col barriers, 1-col revenue cards, 1-col services, stacked process list, 1-col engagement cards.
