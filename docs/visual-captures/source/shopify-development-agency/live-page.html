Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dynamic Dreamz a Shopify development agency?

Yes. Dynamic Dreamz is a Shopify Platinum Partner and Shopify development agency established in 2006. We help brands and digital agencies build, redesign, migrate, integrate and continuously improve Shopify stores.

What Shopify development services does Dynamic Dreamz provide?

We provide custom Shopify store development, Figma/XD to Shopify conversion, theme development and customization, app integrations, migrations, Shopify 2.0 theme upgrades, CRO, performance improvements, maintenance and white-label Shopify development.

Why hire a Shopify development agency instead of one developer?

A larger Shopify project often needs development, design interpretation, QA, project coordination and integration knowledge.
Dynamic Dreamz brings the right mix of specialists around the project instead of expecting one developer to cover every discipline.

Can Dynamic Dreamz build a Shopify store from Figma or customize an existing theme?

Yes. We implement approved Figma/XD designs in Shopify and customize existing themes with reusable sections, product and collection templates, responsive behavior and custom functionality.

Can you migrate Magento, WooCommerce or BigCommerce to Shopify without losing SEO?

We plan migrations around data transfer, URL mapping, 301 redirects, metadata, canonical handling, analytics validation and launch QA. No migration can guarantee unchanged rankings, but SEO preservation is treated as a core part of the replatforming process.

How much does Shopify development cost with Dynamic Dreamz?

Cost depends on scope and engagement model. We offer project-based
custom quotes, flexible Shopify development support from $25/hour, and dedicated Shopify developer or team
options from $2,000/month.

How long does a Shopify development project take?

Timeline depends on design readiness, catalog size, custom functionality, migration requirements and integrations. After reviewing the scope, Dynamic Dreamz provides milestones and a realistic delivery plan rather than applying one timeline to every project.

Does Dynamic Dreamz provide ongoing Shopify maintenance and support?

Yes. We support live Shopify stores with fixes, theme updates, new
features, app changes, CRO implementation, performance improvements, integrations and ongoing development capacity.

Does Dynamic Dreamz provide Shopify Plus development?

Yes. High-growth, B2B and enterprise requirements are supported through our dedicated Shopify Plus Agency capability, including B2B, migrations, integrations, checkout extensibility and ongoing technical support.

Where is Dynamic Dreamz based, and do you work with global Shopify clients?

Dynamic Dreamz is an India-based Shopify development company serving brands and agencies globally. Project communication and delivery are structured around client requirements and working time zones.
